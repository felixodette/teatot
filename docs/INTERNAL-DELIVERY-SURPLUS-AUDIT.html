Internal narrative vs client narrative

Focus: did we ship the restructuring plan? Answer: not yet — Explore missing, home still mixed, nav/slugs wrong. Score ~62; Significant Rework.

Internal gate (this audit)

Focus: is the product system ready to absorb that plan cheaply? Answer: yes — dining/ANAM/services/contact/measurement already exceed; remaining work is mostly IA assembly. Estimated 1–2 weeks to Views Draft green with current assets.

What not to rebuild

  • Do not rebuild Dining — extend home teaser to point at existing /dining + ANAM.
  • Do not invent new attraction copy first — promote existing three posts into /explore; add Wamunyu as fourth card from existing image.
  • Do not replace BookingModal for Views Draft “clarify Book Now” — change label/copy or secondary WA CTA; engine/M-Pesa is a product decision, not a missing page.
  • Do not merge ANAM back into hotel — dual-site is surplus that already satisfies menu depth; only tighten parent-brand cross-links.
  • Do not rewrite Services content — filter/hide restaurant + guest-services presentation; keep conference/garden/catering enquire pattern.
